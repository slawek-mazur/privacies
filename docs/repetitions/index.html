Privacy Policy

Repetitions · Effective 25 July 2026

Repetitions is a spaced repetition learning app. This page explains what data the app stores and why: your notes, your account, and push notifications.

1. Your account

You can sign in with email, Google, or Apple, or use the app anonymously — without an account. An account lets you sync notes across devices; anonymous use ties your notes to that single device only.

2. What we store

  • Your account identifier and email address (if you sign in).
  • The content of your notes: title, description, tag.
  • A device token used to send push notifications.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or data from other apps.

3. How we use this data

Strictly to run the app: syncing your notes, sending reminders about upcoming reviews, and protecting the service from abuse. We don't build advertising profiles and we don't analyse the content of your notes.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. Data is shared only with providers that process it on our behalf so the app can work: Google Firebase (sign-in, notifications) and Google Cloud (hosting the notes database).

5. How long we keep your data

  • Notes and account — until you delete your account.
  • Notification token — until you turn off notifications or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

You can edit or delete your notes at any time, turn off notifications in your system settings, or request account deletion by emailing the contact address below.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If the way we handle data changes, this page will be updated together with the effective date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions and data deletion requests: info@stricte.io.
